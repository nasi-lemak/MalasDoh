MalasDoh 0.2.0 brings themes for the whole app, and a cleaner, easier to read
window.

### Themes

Everything is in the new **Settings → Appearance**.

- **One theme colours everything:** the window, the terminal and the
  editor change together. Built in: Dark, Light, Tomorrow Night, Dracula,
  Gruvbox Dark, Solarized Dark and Solarized Light.
- **Follow system:** switch with your system's dark or light mode, with a
  theme of your choice for each.
- **Your own accent colour** for selections, links and the main button of
  each dialog.
- **Terminal colours** follow the app theme, or keep any palette you like.
- **Make your own themes:** "Copy this theme to a new file" writes the
  current theme out as a small text file of colours. Change it, press
  Reload, and it is in the list. A file with a mistake is left out, with
  the reason shown.
- Colours that mean something (the green and red of a diff, the "as root"
  warning, connection status, search highlights) now come from the theme,
  so they stay readable on light themes too.

If you used "Dark mode" before, nothing changes: you start on the Dark (or
Light) theme, looking as you left it.

### A cleaner look

- **Notices** pop up as a card in the bottom-right corner instead of a line
  of text that was easy to miss.
- **The bottom bar** shows the connection you are looking at and whether it
  is connected; open connections get a coloured dot in the connection list.
- **Dialogs** show their main button in the accent colour, and Delete in
  red.
- **The editor** fills its whole pane, and no longer jumps down when you
  start typing.
- **The terminal** has a small margin from the pane's edge, and search
  results in it are easy to read.
- **With Local files open,** the server's side is labelled "Server: name".
- Roomier buttons, softer corners, and a start screen with keyboard hints.

### Downloads

- **Windows:** the `.zip`, or the `-portable.zip` that keeps everything in a
  folder next to the program.
- **Linux:** the `.deb`, or the `.tar.gz`.
- **macOS:** the universal `.dmg` (Apple Silicon and Intel).

The builds are not code-signed yet: Windows SmartScreen asks before the first
run ("More info", then "Run anyway"), and on macOS right-click the app and
choose Open. `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksums of every file.
